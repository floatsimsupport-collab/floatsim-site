Privacy Policy for Float Simulator 2026

Effective Date: May 19, 2026

Float Simulator 2026 ("the App") is developed by Wesley Hsu / PVIT ROV. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the App to collect no personal information.

Information We Collect

None. Float Simulator 2026 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage statistics, device identifiers, or analytics. The App runs entirely on your device.

Network Connections

The App itself makes no network requests. The only external link is to materovcompetition.org, which opens in your device's default browser when tapped. Your browser's privacy practices apply to that visit; we have no insight into or control over it.

Third-Party Services

Float Simulator 2026 uses no third-party SDKs, ad networks, analytics services, or crash reporters.

Data Storage

No user-generated data is saved between sessions outside of in-memory game state. Closing the App discards your current run.

Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no information whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Permissions

The App requests no system permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, notifications, etc.).

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
